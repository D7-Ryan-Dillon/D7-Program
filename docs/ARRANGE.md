# The Arrange tab (built 2026-10-05)

One building, composed from the tiles, judged as architecture, kept, edited freely, shown and exported. This replaces the earlier plan documents; where the build differs from the plan it says so at the end.

## The rules that shape everything
- **One connected, walkable building.** Every piece must touch the main set (the biggest group of touching pieces) and be reachable on foot from the entrance (a joint with an opening of 4 ft² or more meeting an opening). The generator cannot make an island; manual edits that would cut pieces off open a dialog (remove them too / re-attach them / cancel); Add as tile and every export are blocked while anything is detached or overlapping.
- **Free placement, smart snapping.** Positions are multiples of 0.5 ft (the voxel size), not locked to a 20 ft grid. Near another piece a moved piece snaps flush, then opening to opening, then floor to floor (floor 1 against floor 2 is fine), else the half-foot. **Lattice mode** locks to 10 ft.
- **Any box size.** A tile is a box of whole cells (`tileFt`, any width/depth/height; 20 x 40 x 20 or 20 x 20 x 40 work, tested in `npm run check:arrange`). Tiles from the engine should keep to multiples of 10 ft with 10 ft floor-to-floor so floors line up; Arrange accepts anything. Tiles at another cell size and the Builder's hex prisms cannot be placed (greyed out in the Bank).
- **Orientation**: mirror in X, then quarter turns about the vertical axis (8 orientations), plus optional uniform scale behind the Advanced switch. No tilt or wall mode.

## Screen
Toolbar: undo and redo; Select, Pan (also right-drag, middle-drag, Space-drag or Shift-drag; scroll zooms to the cursor; double-click a surface to turn about that point), Box select, Measure, Entrance; **View** (3D, plan, section, side, fit, cut at a floor) and **Display** (joint marks, smooth model, lattice, compare, visibility, foam see-through) as popups; the arrangement's name, **Add as tile**, **Export**, and a button that hides the side panel. Left: Bank, Generate (shape, priorities; seed, minimum joint and piece scale under More options), Program and limits, Saved arrangements. Right (one tab at a time): **Piece** (the selection), **Info** (Overview with warnings, Joints, Route, Pieces), **Tools** (suggest, fill the gap, replace; smooth and seal; interlock test and pair matrix). The entrance mark and the orange joint plates are drawn only where they help (the entrance mark only while the entrance tool is on).

Global: Alt+1..5 tabs, `[` `]` previous / next tile. ## Checks
`npm run check:arrange` (scripts/check-arrange.ts) runs on the 15 fixtures with no browser: every shape and seed gives one connected, walkable, overlap-free arrangement; never rules are obeyed; turning and mirroring a group are exact; the orphan check agrees with the real check and both repairs work; the composite holds exactly the pieces' cells and reads; smoothing and its approvals; suggestions and replacements are valid; the interlock runs; the pair matrix; non-cubic tiles. Run it with `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm run check:parity`.

## How it differs from the plan, and what is not done
- The Level control is a row of chips (All, L1, L2 ...) that cut the model 4 ft above that floor; the model is cut but not capped.
- The mass sent back to Rhino turns floor plates into ordinary foam (the engine's plate objects are not rebuilt); its flat tops are kept in the voxels.
- The generator runs on the page (about 0.3 s per try, four tries); the whole-building reading runs in a worker. Very large arrangements (over 6 M cells) wait for "Analyse in full".
- Tests of the generator's shape settings check validity, not taste: the shapes are biases, not templates, and a bank with few openings cannot pack tightly.
- The legacy `lib/exporters/csgFuse.ts` and `lib/sections/volumeField.ts` are untouched.
- Not built: a gallery that ranks several arrangements (one is worked on at a time; saved arrangements and compare cover it), a mesh cap on level cuts, per-tab shortcut sets beyond the lists above.
- The drone tour is verified by `npm run check:arrange` (every shape: no sample of the flight inside foam, all pieces visited, 2.5 ft clearance), and played in the live viewport; the recorded MP4 itself was not watched frame by frame.
- Verified in a real browser (Chrome pane): generation, drag, box select, nudge, group turn and the orphan dialog, undo, add, add as tile (it shows in the Viewer), compare, level cut, measure, find views, walk-through MP4, build-up MP4, STL, OBJ, drawings, report, Rhino zip (also ingested by the engine headless), saved list, key bar. Not run in a real browser: GIF export, the Boards tab round trip.

## Pictures, films, boards (round 3)
- **Seed**: every Generate picks a new random seed (and turns the building a new way) unless a seed is kept (More options: typing a seed keeps it).
- **Drone tour** starts and ends on black (a smooth fade over the first and last 0.9 s), so it loops without a join.
- **The best pictures** (zip) have three kinds with their own counts, one size, and a clear-background switch: the whole building from outside, close-ups at the openings on the outside (`exposed` patches), and inside views from places where the view takes in the most different blocks facing each other (rays through the voxels, scored by distinct pieces seen, depth and sky; `findViews` in `lib/arrange/views.ts`). Inside views use the tour look (void hidden, solid foam, a light at the camera).
- **Build-up animation** has two styles: place the blocks one by one (low pieces first, each drops in and fades solid, then its void fades in so the space is made; `buildUpHooks` in `lib/arrange/capture.ts`) or turn the finished model.
- **Text on every canvas export fits its box** (`lib/textBlock.ts`: wrap at words, break a long word, shrink to a minimum, ellipsis last; never squeezed). The report grows in height to show every space and every warning (no "+ N more"); the Analysis table and diagram images and the drawing captions fit the same way.
- **Plans and sections on one board** (`lib/arrange/drawingBoard.ts`): title, key numbers, legend, a scale bar, every plan and section at one scale with its caption; or separate PNGs / SVGs.
- **Colours** (`lib/boardPalette.ts`, `components/shared/PaletteEditor.tsx`): background, text, title, headings, sections, plates, branches, floor tint; saved with the project; used by the report, the board, the separate PNGs, the Viewer drawing export and (with "My colours") the Analysis images.

## Mesh quality in exports
The live viewports show the engine's own meshes (a 40-cell field, light and fast). Pictures, films (drone and build-up), the Viewer and Builder PNG and turntable, the Boards PNG and animation (150 dpi and up), the OBJ files and the STL files swap in smooth meshes rebuilt from each tile's voxels (`lib/fineGeometry.ts`, `lib/exporters/printMesh.ts`: cubic upsampling, surface nets, light smoothing; the box faces stay flat). Each export window has a Mesh quality choice (High by default, Maximum, Standard, or the original engine mesh). The meshes are cached per tile and at most 32 are kept, so repeated exports cannot pile up memory. In the smooth-model view of an arrangement the combined model keeps its own (voxel-grid) meshing.
