# The Analysis tab, against the project's matrix

The authority is Assignment 1 Part 3's matrix (`lib/scoring/matrix.ts`, word for word): twelve descriptors, each with a qualitative criterion, a quantitative criterion and a precedent. Everything else says how the app measures or approximates it, and says so. The whole thing is automatic: loading a tile (or adding an arrangement as a tile) reads it against the matrix, suggests the criteria to carry forward, fills the comparison and the boards. Every control is an optional override.

## What each result carries
`lib/scoring/matrixEval.ts` `evaluateTile(tile, inputs)` returns, for each descriptor: the value with its unit and a one-line result; a **status** (measured, inferred, proxy, assumed, not assessable, not applicable); how it was measured (what was counted, the denominator, the thresholds); **what it cannot establish**; an automated reading (labelled generated, never a human judgment or a simulation); the assumptions it used; and evidence (rooms, levels, a route, regions) for the views and the diagrams. Missing information gives "not assessable" with the reason, never a zero or a pass; the rest of the workflow carries on. The app's older 0-100 blend is kept as a small "presence index", labelled as such; it is not the matrix measurement and is not used to choose criteria. Old scores are not relabelled: Spatial density is not a matrix descriptor (the twelfth is Compressed-then-released); it is kept as a supplemental reading and its scores are not converted.

## How each descriptor is read (and what is only a proxy)
| Descriptor | Quantity | Status | Method in one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carved | % of void surface seamless vs paneled | proxy | faces of the void against eroded foam over all faces against material (foam, plates, branches); the files hold no panel model, so seams cannot be counted; triangles and voxel steps are never seams |
| Stepped / Terraced | setback increments per building height | measured | steps of the void's section (layers within 1 ft are one stage; a stage must hold 3 ft, a step moves 2 ft) and terraces of floors; per 10 ft of height; the container's own setbacks reported beside |
| Porous | % of void wall area that is opening | measured | wall faces of the void with air beyond over openings plus solid wall (rough wall scaled to the smooth surface); bridges and cross-level void listed, not added |
| Continuous | seams per 10 m of surface | proxy / not assessable | material changes (foam ↔ plate or branch) on the floor and both walls along a floor-supported route, per 10 m; not assessable without a route; circulation connectivity is not substituted |
| Retained / Resistant | % of floor area / volume that is retained solid | measured / not assessable | the retained element is the recipe's protected plates and their branches; area is plan area over the container's footprint, volume over the container's cells; with no plates nothing is called retained |
| Threaded | public program instances above ground | assumed / not assessable | rooms above the ground floor whose program (tile category, or for an assembly each room's own tile) is on the visible list of public categories; any room can be set by hand; no category, no assumption |
| Graduated | enclosure steps | proxy | enclosure class (open to the sky, partly open, enclosed) along a floor-supported route from the outside; private/public is not assigned |
| Non-hierarchical Circulation | distinct routes entry → destination | measured | cheapest floor-supported route, then its corridor closed off and again, up to a limit; entry, destination, corridor width and limit are stated |
| Force-driven | measurable generating input | measured / not assessable | the recipe's dose, gravity, drain and plate behaviour, and void made per dose; without a recipe nothing is inferred; does not claim a legible force |
| Light-filled | skylight area / void floor below | proxy | roof opening (void with air straight above) over the floor of the rooms it opens into; unglazed in the model; a geometric indicator, not a simulation; an assembly recomputes it from the combined voxels, so a tile above removes it |
| Monumental | height-to-width ratio, void share of building volume | measured | tallest room's clear height over its narrower extent, with actual dimensions; volume over the container's own volume |
| Compressed-then-released | narrowest ÷ widest passage width | measured / not assessable | clear width across the direction of travel, at standing height, along a floor-supported route, smoothed over 3 ft; constrictions and expansions located; does not assume more contrast is better |

A **floor-supported route** (`lib/scoring/voxelFacts.ts`): cells with material under them and headroom, joined by steps no bigger than 0.5 ft, from the side of the container with the most ground-level opening to a destination (the middle of the largest other room, or the farthest point reached on foot). If there is none, the criteria that need one say so; no line through open air is substituted.

**Non-cubic containers and assemblies**: everything is read from the cells of the container (`mask.u8`), so the notch of an L is open air, never foam or void, and never part of a denominator.

## Usable space (`lib/scoring/usable.ts`, `UsablePanel`)
Carved void against the void over floor that can be reached on foot; plate area against floor that is exposed and usable (and what is too narrow or too low, and what has no way to it); void pieces against floor zones joined to the way in; void links between levels against levels reached on foot. The thresholds are Arrange's (6.5 ft headroom, 2.5 ft clear width, 0.5 ft steps): proto-architectural, not structural, accessibility or code checks. Problems are shown where they are and can be lit on the plans.

## The profile, the criteria and the comparison (`lib/scoring/profile.ts`, `lib/useEvaluation.ts`)
- The criteria carried forward are **suggested automatically** from the whole set (can it be assessed, how good is the evidence, does it tell the tiles apart, are the typologies about it, does it repeat another) and adopted at once. **No minimum number.** A tile's score is never used: criteria are not chosen because they make tiles look good.
- New tiles are read against the adopted profile immediately. If the tiles change what would be suggested, the change is **shown** (what to add, what to remove, why) and takes effect only when you press Adopt; every tile is then read the same way again.
- **Overrides** (all optional, each restorable on its own, all saved with the project): pin a criterion on or off, the reason text, an interpretation you wrote (per tile), any threshold or assumption, a room's class, the route a tile is read along, the tile picked for a typology and your reason, the typology's spatial intention.
- Variants of a typology are read with the same criteria and assumptions, raw values and generated readings side by side. A suggested selection is made only where the evidence separates them (two or more criteria the typology is about, one variant leading clearly); otherwise it says the evidence does not support a pick. The fifteen typologies are never ranked against each other.
- Older projects: pins and reasons you wrote are kept as overrides; the old carried list is not converted (it was chosen from the older scores) and a fresh suggestion replaces it; a note says what happened, and Spatial density is not turned into Compressed-then-released.

## Boards and exports
The Analysis tab, the Boards descriptor page, the Analysis sheets, the results table (CSV and image), the annotated diagrams and the arrangement report all read the same `evaluateTile` results (`lib/scoring/boardText.ts` writes the board text; `lib/scoring/exportResults.ts` the table and diagrams). **Analysis sheets** (Boards → Export): landscape pages on the board's background, 22 × 11 in by default (any size, any dpi), automatic: (1) typology, spatial intention and the matrix in its own words with what is carried and why; (2) measurements with units and status and the readings (yours marked ✎), several variants side by side; (3) evidence diagrams, the usable-space check, the suggested selection and its rationale. Each kind of content can be switched off.

## Tested without a browser
`npm run check:analysis`: the twelve descriptors word for word; every ordinary tile reads with no manual input; unlabelled program, no plates, no recipe, no voxels and a sealed room are "not assessable" with a reason; Continuous is never "measured" and does not use circulation; Compressed-then-released on a known passage (a 2 ft neck between a 4 ft passage and a 10 ft chamber reads 0.24, the neck and chamber located); non-cubic denominators (the L's 3,000 ft³ and 300 ft², not the box's); an assembly where a block over a roof opening removes the skylight and a gathering room above a lobby is one public instance above ground; overrides persist through a JSON round trip and reset one at a time; a profile stays put as variants arrive; a pick is not forced; migration; the CSV, the Boards text and the Analysis are the same numbers. `scripts/render-analysis.ts` draws the sheets, the table and the diagrams outside a browser (with `npm i --no-save @napi-rs/canvas`) so they can be looked at.

## Limits
Not run in a real browser (the Analysis tab's new panels and the Boards sheet panel): type check, lint, build and the checks above pass, and the sheets were drawn and looked at in node. Seams, panels, glazing, program labels beyond the tile category, required sun-hours and pedestrian counts are not in the files, so Continuous, Carved and Light-filled are proxies and Force-driven reads only the recipe. Enclosure steps do not assign private and public. One entry and one destination are read per tile (the largest ground-level opening; override in Settings). The selection suggestion weighs only carried criteria that a typology's name makes relevant; it is a suggestion to look at.
